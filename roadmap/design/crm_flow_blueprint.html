CRM Flow Blueprint

One intake mouth. One shared spine. Typed pipelines. One handoff. Every enquiry — IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Meta, website, cold call, exhibition, walk-in — enters the same funnel mouth, is deduped against Omni Identity, gets classified into a customer type, and then runs a pipeline shaped for that type: tight gates where money and compliance are at stake, open configuration everywhere else. Won deals exit through a single atomic handoff into ERPNext. Excom carries the conversation across all of it and never changes owner.

The Six Pipelines

Solid chips are customer-facing stages. Dashed chips are internal gates (the customer is not waiting on themselves — you are). ⊘ marks a hard gate: the deal cannot advance until it clears. Filled chip = terminal.

Distributor

cycle 2–6 wkowner: channel salesdeal SLA: touch every 3 d

Highest-value relationship. The gate that matters is territory — one bad grant poisons a region.

Qualified→ Territory Check→ Pitch + Price Slab→ Sample Kit→ Negotiation→ Agreement + Onboarding→ First Order · Won
  • Territory gate: no active distributor conflict on the proposed pin codes; overlap needs a named manager's override, logged.
  • Onboarding gate: GST verified, signed agreement, security deposit received, credit limit approved — all four before Won.
handoffCustomer group=Distributor · territory · distributor price list · credit limit → fieldforce maps outlets under them

Retailer

cycle: daysowner: telecaller / fieldlightest pipeline

Not really one pipeline — a routing decision. The channel model (secondary sales, HLD A1) decides direct vs distributor.

Qualified→ Territory Route→ branch:
Territory covered by a distributor
Hand off: create Outlet under the serving distributor, notify them via excom, close deal as Won — Routed. The distributor's SR picks them up on the beat.
Territory uncovered
Serve direct: retail price list → first order → Won — Direct. Flag the pin code as distributor-gap intel for channel sales.
  • Channel-conflict gate: a covered-territory retailer is never quietly sold direct — that undercuts the distributor you just onboarded.
handoffOutlet + distributor mapping — or — Customer group=Retailer, retail price list

Export / International Importer

cycle 4–16 wkowner: export deskresponse < 24 h (time zones)

Compliance is a stage, not an afterthought — certifications differ per destination, and the compliance team talks through the same excom thread.

Qualified→ Spec Confirmed→ Compliance Check→ Export Quote→ Sample Shipment→ Negotiation→ Pro Forma→ Advance / LC · Won
  • Compliance gate: destination-country certifications confirmed feasible before any quote goes out.
  • Payment gate: quote states Incoterms + currency; Won only on verified advance or LC draft accepted.
handoffCustomer multi-currency · payment terms · Incoterms default → Quotation/Sales Order in ERPNext Desk

Corporate Gifting

cycle 1–4 wkowner: gifting deskdeadline-driven

The event date is the boss. Every stage is reverse-scheduled from it; a deal that can't make the date is disqualified early, politely, with a next-occasion nudge.

Qualified (event date + qty + budget)→ Curation + Mockup→ Quote→ Approval→ Advance→ In Production · Won
  • Date-feasibility gate: at Qualified, production lead time + logistics must fit before the event date, or the deal exits as Lost — Timeline.
  • Advance gate: branding/customised production never starts before advance payment clears.
handoffCustomer group=Corporate · Sales Order with delivery date = event date − buffer. Seasonal: excom broadcast to past gifting customers 8 wk before Diwali / New Year.

OEM — Private Label + Custom Designed (one pipeline, one branch field)

cycle 6–20 wkowner: OEM desk + productiondesign_by: customer | us

Private label ("put our brand on your product") and custom designed ("make this thing we specced / design it for us") share 80% of their flow. One pipeline with a design_by branch keeps it tight; splitting them creates two half-maintained pipelines. The sampling loop is bounded — unbounded sampling is where OEM deals go to die.

Qualified→ NDA / Brief→ Spec + Design (theirs or ours)→ Feasibility + Costing→ Sampling Loop (≤ 3 rounds)→ Commercial Terms→ Contract / PO · Won
  • Feasibility gate (internal): production signs off MOQ, tooling cost, and lead time before the customer ever sees a price. A quote production can't honour is worse than a slow quote.
  • Sampling gate: each round has written feedback; after round 3 the deal escalates to a call — approve, one paid round, or part ways.
  • Commercial gate: tooling/plate charges and MOQ agreed in writing before contract.
handoffCustomer group=OEM · item codes created for the custom SKUs · Sales Order with tooling as a separate line

Online B2C — the bypass

cycle: minutesno deal created

An individual buying one unit is not a pipeline — putting them in one buries the real deals. B2C enquiries skip CRM entirely.

Classified B2C→ Route to store / order capture→ Excom thread only
  • Promotion rule: if a "B2C" buyer asks about bulk, dealership, or branding — one click promotes the thread to a typed Lead with provenance intact.
handoffOrder via store/marketplace; identity + thread retained for support and repeat-purchase campaigns

Guardrails

The rules that keep the funnel honest. Tight here, open everywhere else.

R1Repeat enquiry ≠ new lead

Dedupe (S2) matches phone/email against Omni Identity. An existing customer enquiring again opens their excom thread and pings the account owner — it never creates a duplicate lead. An open lead getting a second-source touch merges provenance into the existing lead (first touch wins, later touches logged).

R2Provenance is written once

source · campaign · channel · first_touch_at · first_touch_by stamp at capture and are immutable. Attribution must survive to retention (HLD-002), so nobody "corrects" the source after the deal wins.

R3No untyped deals

Lead→Deal conversion requires customer_type. Kanban views, required fields, SLA profile, and owner team all key off it. "Unknown" is a lead state, never a deal state.

R4Assignment: sticky, then team

Assignment Rules route by customer_type × territory. If the Omni Identity has a prior owner, they get it first (sticky); else round-robin within the type's team — mirroring excom Phase C call routing, so calls and deals land on the same human.

R5Won is atomic

The conversion contract from HLD-002 §Stage 3 applies to every pipeline: Customer created/located, Omni Identity re-pointed with CRM links retained, provenance stamped, deal locked, system message posted to the thread. No half-converted deals.

R6Lost has a reason, always

Closed-lost requires a reason from a short, per-type list (Price, Timeline, Territory conflict, Spec infeasible, Unresponsive, Chose competitor). Free-text is allowed in addition, never instead. This is the raw material for every future "why are we losing importers?" question.

Open Decisions

Flagged for discussion before build — each changes the flow above if answered differently.

Q1OEM merge

This blueprint merges Private Label and Custom Designed into one pipeline with a design_by branch. Split only if the two are owned by different teams or priced by different logic.

Q2Retailer channel policy

The covered-territory retailer routes to the distributor, never direct. Is that the actual commercial policy, or do some product lines sell direct-to-retail even in covered territories?

Q3Export quoting home

HLD-002 A4 flags this: if export needs ERPNext-native quoting (items, taxes, currency) from day one, the Export Quote and Pro Forma stages live in ERPNext with the deal tracking status only. Confirm which side owns the quote document.

Q4IndiaMART subscription shape

Direct enquiries and purchased Buy Leads have different intent quality. If both are in play, they should be distinct CRM Lead Source rows with different SLA and different junk expectations.
