Privacy Policy — {{ company }} customer messaging

Effective 2026-09-04 · applies to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, Meta lead forms, email and web chat conversations with {{ company }}.

Who we are

{{ company }} operates this site ({{ site }}). We use Meta Platforms' Business tools (WhatsApp Business Platform, Instagram Messaging, Messenger Platform and Meta Lead Ads) to talk to customers and prospects who contact us or submit a lead form.

What we collect

  • Your name / profile name, phone number, email address and the platform identifier Meta assigns to you (for example a Page-scoped or Instagram-scoped id).
  • The content of messages you send us and we send you, including attachments, and message delivery status.
  • Lead form answers you submit on Facebook or Instagram (name, phone, email, company, city and any custom questions).
  • Technical details needed to route the conversation: timestamps, the page or number you contacted, campaign or form name.

Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry, quote, take orders and provide support.
  • To keep one conversation history per contact across channels so you do not repeat yourself.
  • To record the enquiry as a lead or customer in our ERP for follow-up and accounting.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use Meta data for advertising audiences or profiling beyond serving your enquiry.

Retention

Conversation history is retained as part of our customer records. Raw lead-form and webhook payloads are purged automatically after 90 days. Data received from Meta is deleted on request (see below).

Sharing

Data stays in our self-hosted ERP. It is shared only with the platform you contacted us on (Meta / WhatsApp) to deliver replies, and with our staff handling your enquiry.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete data we received from Meta. Write to {{ contact_email or "our contact address" }}, or use the deletion instructions at {{ site }}/excom-data-deletion.
